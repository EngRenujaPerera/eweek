Rules & Regulations

  • You can write in your native language (Sinhala, Tamil, or English).
  • You should choose one of the official topics provided below for your selected medium.
  • You can submit traditional poems or free verse. Both will be evaluated and selected separately.
  • All poems and free verse must include an original title.
  • Length criteria: 20–30 lines for traditional poems; 10–20 lines for free verse.
  • There is no limit on the number of participants who can participate from each batch.
  • Your submission must include your full name and batch.
  • All entries must be original works. Plagiarism will lead to immediate disqualification.
  • Final decisions will be determined by the panel of judges.
  • Winners (1st, 2nd, and 3rd places) earn points contributing toward batch standings.
