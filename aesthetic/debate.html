Instructions & Rules

  • Each batch shall be represented by exactly one team consisting of four eligible members.
  • Three debate topics will be provided to all participating teams one week prior to the competition.
  • The draw to determine affirmative and opposing positions takes place on the day of the tournament.
  • If a second round is conducted, topics will be announced on the spot with 20 minutes preparation time.
  • Only one participant may speak at a time. Side discussions and interruptions are strictly prohibited.
  • Participants must directly address and counter opposing arguments, without shifting to unrelated points.
  • Sources for all statistics, quotations, and surveys must be acknowledged orally when referenced.
  • Handwritten notes and question slips are permitted; however, all electronic devices (phones, tablets, laptops, smartwatches) are strictly banished from preparation and chamber areas.
  • Evaluation criteria: Relevance & Logic, Fluency, Team Participation, Grammatical Accuracy, and Vocabulary.

Speech Order & Duration Protocol

Order of Speeches: AF1 → OP1 → AF2 → OP2 → AF3 → OP3 → AF4 → OP4 → OP1 Summary → AF1 Summary
First Speakers (AF1 & OP1): 5 Minutes Speech + 2 Minutes Final Summary
Subsequent Speakers (AF2–4, OP2–4): 4 Minutes Speech Each
Prohibited Devices: All Electronics (Phones, Laptops, Wearables)
