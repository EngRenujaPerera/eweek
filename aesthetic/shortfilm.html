Rules & Regulations

  • Anyone from the Faculty of Engineering can participate, either individually or as a team.
  • Every short film must include English subtitles.
  • Films submitted or showcased in last year's competition cannot be entered again.
  • The film must be original work. Any copyrighted material requires verified permission.
  • Maximum duration is 10 minutes, including opening and closing credits.
  • Submissions must be in HD resolution, MP4 format (H.264 video, AAC stereo audio).
  • Each individual or team can submit only one film.
  • There is no fixed theme or genre; any creative storyline is permitted.
  • Content must be suitable for all audiences, strictly free of offensive or explicit material.
  • AI assistance and visual effects are permitted; however, generating AI characters is strictly prohibited.
  • Films must be submitted through the official link and handed over on a physical pen drive.
  • Each film must provide the director's name, contact details, and a brief synopsis.
  • There will be separate winners awarded for Sinhala and Tamil categories. The judges' decision is final.

Technical Delivery Specifications

Duration: Up to 10 Minutes (inc. credits)
Video Format: HD MP4 (H.264 Codec)
Audio Format: AAC Stereo
Subtitles: English Subtitles Mandatory
