Instructions & Rules

  • A maximum of two participants from each batch may register for the E-Speaker Competition.
  • The competition consists of two rounds, and all registered participants must take part in both rounds.
  • Round 1 (Prepared Speech): Each participant chooses one topic from the 10 official topics provided below. Speaking duration is 8 minutes. Visual aids and props (PowerPoint presentations, posters, physical models) are permitted.
  • Round 2 (Impromptu Speech): Topics will be announced on the spot. Participants will receive 30 minutes of preparation time and an allocated 5-minute speaking delivery window.
  • Speeches must be delivered in a respectful, professional, and disciplined manner. Offensive language, inappropriate expressions, or discriminatory remarks are strictly prohibited.
  • Participants must strictly adhere to the time limits (8 mins for Round 1, 5 mins for Round 2) without exceeding allocated speaking times.
  • Evaluation criteria: Content & Structure (30%), Delivery (30%), Audience Connection (20%), Use of Visuals (10%), and Time Management (10%).

Rounds & Evaluation Framework

Round 1: 8-Minute Prepared Speech
Round 2: 5-Minute Impromptu (30 mins prep)
